# Eye Saver

Eye Saver is a parent-controlled Android app for creating healthier screen-time routines on a shared phone.

- [Privacy policy](privacy/)
- [Support and issue reporting](https://github.com/manoj020218/eye_saver/issues)

Eye Saver is a wellbeing tool, not a medical device. Distance estimates are approximate and are not medical advice.
