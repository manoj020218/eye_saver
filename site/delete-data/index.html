Delete your Eye Saver data

Eye Saver has no account and no server. Everything it stores lives only on your phone, so you can delete it completely yourself.

Delete all data and keep the app

  1. Open Eye Saver and stop any active child session with your parent PIN.
  2. Open Android Settings → Apps → Eye Saver → Storage.
  3. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data).

This removes the parent PIN, session history, selected apps, calibration and any face-template data. Keys held in Android Keystore for the app are removed as well.

Delete all data and remove the app

  1. Stop any active child session with your parent PIN.
  2. If Device Admin protection was enabled, turn it off in Settings → Security → Device admin apps → Eye Saver.
  3. Uninstall Eye Saver. Android deletes all of its data.
The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete remotely. Eye Saver disables Android backup, so no copy is kept in your Google account backup either.
