Eye Saver

Parent-controlled screen time, app breaks and private viewing-distance reminders for a shared Android phone.

Eye Saver gives parents one simple place to choose a session length, select the apps covered when time ends, and start a protected child session. The timer counts only active screen time — it pauses when the display is off.

Parent PIN

A locally created PIN protects settings and is required to stop an active child session.

App breaks

When time runs out, a full-screen break covers only the apps the parent selected. Calls stay available.

Distance reminders

Optional front-camera analysis warns when the phone is held too close. Frames never leave the phone.

Tamper protection

Optional protection of Settings and app installers during child mode, with reboot recovery.

Private by design. No ads, no account, no analytics and no cloud upload. The release app does not even request Internet access. Read the privacy policy.

Who it is for

Eye Saver is set up and controlled by a parent or legal guardian (18+). It is a wellbeing tool, not a medical device; camera distance estimates are approximate and are not medical advice.
