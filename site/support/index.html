Frequently asked questions

Why does Eye Saver ask for so many permissions?

Each one powers a specific protection: usage access detects which app is open, "display over other apps" shows the break screen, notifications keep active protection visible, and the battery exemption stops Android from closing the timer. Quick setup in the app opens each settings page for you. Every permission is explained in the privacy policy.

The break screen does not appear on my phone.

Open Eye Saver → Setup and make sure every item is green. On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Samsung and similar phones, also allow AutoStart / background activity for Eye Saver, otherwise the system may stop it.

Can my child still make phone calls?

Yes. Only apps you select are covered when time runs out. Leave the Phone app unselected to keep calls available.

Is the camera recording my child?

No. Distance monitoring is optional. Camera frames are analysed in memory on the phone, never saved, and never uploaded — the app has no Internet permission. Android shows its camera indicator while monitoring is on.

I forgot my parent PIN.

For security there is no remote reset, because Eye Saver has no account or server. Deactivate Eye Saver in Android Settings → Security → Device admin apps (if enabled), then clear the app's storage or reinstall it and create a new PIN.

How do I uninstall Eye Saver?

Stop any active session with your parent PIN, turn off Eye Saver in Device admin apps (if enabled), then uninstall normally.

How accurate is the distance warning?

It is an approximate estimate that depends on face, angle, lighting and camera. Run the guided calibration for the child's profile for best results. Eye Saver is not a medical device.
